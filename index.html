I'm Nivedkrishna Thavarayil — a director and writer first, and the entrepreneur behind two companies. Based between New Jersey and New York City, I work across a footprint that spans NJ, NYC, Philadelphia, LA, Bangalore, and Delhi. I founded Thava Studio (operating as Nivedkrishna LLC), a creative studio delivering social strategy, content production, and web development for restaurants, real estate, healthcare, and entertainment clients across the US, and I co-founded Growvior Software, a software and AI product company out of Kochi, Bangalore, and New York building SaaS platforms, websites, and apps — including our in-house EdTech product, Mockits.

My background blends 5+ years of brand strategy, viral social campaigns, and now software go-to-market with a filmmaker's eye. I cut my teeth as part of the 15-person founding team at WLDD in Bangalore, where I was the first person to pitch and close Sony Pictures as an international client — a relationship that grew into a standing retainer across The Pope's Exorcist, 65, Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse, and Insidious: The Red Door. Across two years there I led or contributed to 50+ campaigns for brands including Durex, Swiggy, Zomato, Bingo! Mad Angles, Xiaomi, OnePlus, Zee5, Netflix India, Prime Video, and Veera Browser, and grew the agency's own Instagram from 2K to 11K followers.

Today that same range shows up in Thava Studio's client list: shooting and strategizing content for restaurants and hospitality brands (Frangos Peri-Peri, Sam & Sha Inc's restaurant group, Sapphire Banquet NY), running channels for entertainers (mentalist Michael Samen), building and marketing real estate and healthcare brands (RealtorLevin, YourPrimaryCare), and pitching Growvior's software and AI products to institutions and startups — all while directing and writing my own films (NYFA Dean's List 2025) and running a 24K+ organic following built with zero ad spend.

Currently running Thava Studio with 18+ active clients across New York, New Jersey, Philadelphia, Long Island, LA, Bangalore, and Delhi — alongside co-founding Growvior Software.
